Inquiries

Common Alignments

Answers to technical, pricing, and structural questions about working with Cygnus Automation.

What exactly is an "AI Agent" and how does it work?

An AI Agent is a custom software program running on cutting-edge Large Language Models (LLMs) that is trained to autonomously perform complex workflows. Unlike simple triggers, it can read data contextually, search databases, make conditional adjustments, draft communications, and utilize APIs depending on your business parameters.

How do you price custom automation setups?

We work on a custom project-scoped basis or structured monthly retainer plans. Because AI architectures differ significantly depending on API access requirements, volume of logs processed, and database custom structures, we provide a concrete scoping proposal following your Free Automation Audit.

How long does an automation development cycle take?

A standard mid-scale automation (e.g. Lead Enrichment pipelines or automated CRM synchronization loops) is designed, tested, and fully deployed within 3 to 5 weeks. More complex agents requiring deep proprietary knowledge training take between 6 to 8 weeks.

Will these automations integrate with our existing toolset?

Yes. We build connections directly on your existing stack, including common platforms (HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Gmail, Airtable, Notion) as well as custom-coded backend databases, legacy applications, and proprietary internal endpoints via highly secure API standards.

Is our sensitive company data and logs secure?

Absolutely. Data security is our absolute standard. We enforce SOC2-compliant protocols, configure data encryption standards for credentials, and structure AI prompts so that your proprietary business records are never utilized in public LLM fine-tuning datasets.
